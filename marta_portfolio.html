3D Animation - Cybercity


The main character appears in a futuristic city, wandering through buildings and observing the environment. This journey is portrayed through a subjective perspective (POV), interspersed with wide shots of the urban landscape. The aesthetics were influenced by the Cyberpunk 2077 universe, particularly in terms of the density of buildings, artificial lighting, and the feeling of intense urbanization.

3D Animation - Yotsuki


The main character is an original creation (OC) named Yotsuki, who is the central element of the animation. The video is based on a pre-existing story, but I chose not to explicitly develop the concept or narrative, as it would not be noticeable. So, I limited myself to subtly introducing the character in one of the main scenes.

My Room


In this exercise, the goal was to create a 3D environment in Blender that represented 'my room.' For this project, I opted for a simpler and more delicate approach. The modeled room is a small representation of how I idealize my personal space, a reflection of my tastes and preferences.
