Cybercity

The main character appears in a futuristic city, wandering through buildings and observing the environment. This journey is portrayed through a subjective perspective (POV), interspersed with wide shots of the urban landscape. The aesthetics were influenced by the Cyberpunk 2077 universe, particularly in terms of the density of buildings, artificial lighting, and the feeling of intense urbanization.

The character is my OC, used as a reference for the project.
